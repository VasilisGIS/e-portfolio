##Welcome to my portfolio!

###My Background
I am a Master’s student of Applied Geoinformatics at the university of Salzburg, Austria, having previously graduated with a bachelor’s degree in Wildlife Management and Engineering from the Hungarian university of Agriculture and Life sciences (MATE). My academic journey has been formulated by two interests that developed independently throughout my life: wildlife and technology.
From a very young age, I was exposed to the wilderness through my father, an avid hunter who frequently brought me along in his expeditions. Through these experiences I became deeply fascinated with the natural world and this fascination soon grew into a passion for wildlife conservation and management. My interest in wildlife is not limited to terrestrial ecosystems as I enjoy free diving and exploring marine environments.
###Early interest in programming
Along side my passion for nature, I developed an early curiosity about programming. As a child, I wanted to create a Minecraft server where my friends and I could enjoy the custom minigames(plugins) offered by large servers. However, those plugins were not publicly available that’s why I opted to search on GitHub for older, often outdated projects and attempted to update or repair them to work on my server. Although this experienced was short lived, it introduced me to programming and sparked an interest for software development.
###Academic Journey
During my undergraduate studies I had the opportunity to partake in 3 internships across different areas of environmental of environmental and wildlife management. In Sicily, I contributed in monitoring the condition of Posidonia/seagrass meadows with project MANAIA. In Transylvania, I gained practical experience in wildlife game management, while in Cyprus, I joined the wildlife research team of the game and fauna service and worked on conservation and monitoring projects.
###Discovering Geoinformatics
My experience in Cyprus was particularly influential in formulating my academic direction. During my internship I had the opportunity to participate in fitting GNSS tracking devices to European turtle doves (Streptopelia turtur) and analyze their migration patterns through Geographic information systems (GIS) for my bachelor’s thesis. I became particularly interested on how programming and automation can simplify complex workflows and improve efficiency.
For me geoinformatics is an opportunity to combine my interests of the natural world and technology.
